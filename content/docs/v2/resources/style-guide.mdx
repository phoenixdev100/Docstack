This page documents every component available in `.mdx` files. It's the authoring reference - if a kind of information needs a home, one of these patterns fits it.

## Steps

Use `<Steps>`/`<Step>` for sequential tutorials - numbering is automatic.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    `POST /projects` with a name and region. The response gives you a `prj_` ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register a webhook endpoint">
    Point an `https://` URL at your handler and subscribe to `project.updated`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the signature">
    HMAC-SHA256 over `timestamp.body` - see the [webhooks guide](/docs/guides/webhooks).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frontmatter status flags

Pages support `badge: "Beta"` and `deprecated: true` in frontmatter - the badge renders next to the title and deprecated pages get an automatic warning banner. Individual params take `deprecated: true` in `ParamTable`.

<details>
<summary>Raw frontmatter example</summary>

```yaml
---
title: Legacy endpoints
description: v1 compatibility surface.
badge: Deprecated
deprecated: true
updatedAt: 2026-04-01
---
```

</details>
