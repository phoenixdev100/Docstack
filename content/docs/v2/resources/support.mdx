## Channels

| Channel | Best for | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Docs + search** | Most questions - you're here | Everyone |
| **Email** - support@meridian.dev | Bugs, account issues, how-does-X-work | Everyone |
| **In-dashboard chat** | Quick questions while you're working | All paid plans |
| **Priority support** | Production-impacting issues | Team & Enterprise |
| **Dedicated Slack channel** | Enterprise accounts - shared channel with our engineers | Enterprise |
| **security@meridian.dev** | Vulnerabilities, key compromise | Everyone - always |

## Response targets

| Severity | First response | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical - production down | < 1 hour | Enterprise; < 4h Team |
| Major - feature unusable, workaround exists | < 4 hours | Paid plans |
| Standard - questions, minor bugs | < 1 business day | Paid plans |
| Community tier | Best effort | Free |

Critical = your production system can't function. Please mark it accurately - crying wolf on a critical gets triaged, not skipped.

## Security issues - always

- **Key leaked?** Revoke it *first* (`DELETE /keys/:id` or dashboard), then email - rotation takes seconds, forensics can wait.
- **Vulnerability?** security@meridian.dev - see [Responsible disclosure](/docs/security/overview#responsible-disclosure). Don't post publicly first.

## What to include

Reports resolve fastest with:

- **`Meridian-Request-Id`** - the single most useful artifact; it's our trace ID
- `error.code` (not just the message)
- Environment: `test` or `live`
- SDK + version (if applicable) - `meridian --version`, package.json, etc.
- Expected vs actual behavior, minimal repro if you have one

<Callout type="tip">
  A report with a `request_id` usually gets a real answer in the first reply - we can pull the exact request path instead of guessing from a description.
</Callout>

## Status and incidents

- **[status.meridian.dev](https://status.meridian.dev)** - real-time component status + incident history
- Subscribe to updates (email/webhook) - don't email support during a visible incident; we're on it
- Postmortems are published for SEV-1/SEV-2 incidents

## Self-serve first

Most questions land in one of these:

- [Troubleshooting](/docs/resources/troubleshooting) - symptom → fix
- [Known issues](/docs/resources/known-issues) - tracked bugs + workarounds
- [FAQ](/docs/resources/faq) - common platform questions
- [Glossary](/docs/getting-started/glossary) - terminology
- [Changelog](/docs/changelog) - what changed recently

## Feature requests

We take them seriously - the backlog is shaped by reports:

- Describe the *problem*, not the solution - "I need to X because Y" beats "add a Z flag"
- Include your use case - scale, constraints, what you've tried
- Votes matter - if it's already requested, +1 it rather than filing a duplicate

## Escalation

If a response isn't working: reply asking for escalation, or (Enterprise) ping your account manager. Include the thread history - you shouldn't have to re-explain.
